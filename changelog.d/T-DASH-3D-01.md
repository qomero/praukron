- Dashboard 3D view: Work gains a third view beside the lanes and the force
  view, the **3D view**, an immersive view to explore the project rather than
  the operational one. Phases are stacked floors, modules cluster on their
  floor, and decisions and assumptions float near what they bear on. It draws
  the same nodes, lines, selection, filters, search and side panel as the other
  views, with the same shapes and words; far modules fold into a counted bubble,
  and labels never print over one another while the selection, its chain and
  waiting items are always labelled. `3` opens it. By default it turns by itself
  on two axes, one vertical turn in about 60 seconds and one tilt turn for every
  two; `m` switches to a turn on one axis, `p` pauses, `[` and `]` change the
  speed, drag orbits, the wheel or `+` `-` `0` zoom, and Shift with an arrow
  pans. Resting the pointer on a node moves the view toward it, which a button
  turns off. Settings are remembered, and a stored choice wins over the
  default. With reduced motion it is drawn still and no view change is
  animated. Nothing of it is computed before it first opens, no frame is drawn
  while it is not shown, and it uses no library and no network.
